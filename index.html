Friendship Club is a time for Jefferson students to play, talk and make real friends. Fifth-grade student leaders, students with and without disabilities, and grown-ups all take part. Everyone has something to share, and everyone belongs.

Our first gathering, November 4 Done

Our student helpers came, and friends from Room 12 joined us too. Students started playing games, invited each other in, and made new connections on their own.

Create the space. Invite everyone. Support every way of communicating. Let friendships grow.

The Friendship Foundation is a group that helps kids and grown-ups with disabilities make lasting friendships, grow more independent and take part in their community. It also trains volunteers to be friends and leaders.

It runs programs like social and fun activities, summer camps, and Friendship School Clubs. Our Jefferson club shares the same big ideas: friendship, inclusion and community.
